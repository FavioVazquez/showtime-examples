## hook
This bee is giving directions. Her dance tells the colony where the food is.
<!-- F1, F2 (the clip: a trained forager dancing for a feeder; "food" is true for a feeder) -->

## problem
Inside the hive it's dark, and the comb is a vertical wall. She can't point at the flowers from in here. So she dances.
<!-- F3 (framing only; no general "bees can't point" claim) -->

## angle
On the comb, straight up means toward the sun. Food forty-five degrees right of the sun? Her waggle run leans forty-five degrees right of straight up. As the sun moves, she shifts her angle to match.
<!-- F4, F5 (the article's own 45 degree example), F6 -->

## distance
She repeats the loop, turning left, then right: a figure-eight. The waggle run carries the distance. The farther the food, the longer she waggles.
<!-- F8, F7 (relative only: no seconds or metres) -->

## round
For very close food, about ten metres, she runs in a small circle instead. Past about forty metres, the circle stretches into the waggle dance.
<!-- F9 (species qualifier on screen: Italian honey bee, A. m. ligustica) -->

# tidepool-trailer: studio brief

Current truth for this job. Rewrite sections in place as decisions land; keep it to one screen.
The history lives in decisions.md. The build reads this file.

## Contract
This 20 s trailer tells people who keep notes that everything they write in Tidepool stays on
their own machine, and lets them see it before they hear the name. Plays muted-first in feeds
(X, LinkedIn) and as the README header of the showtime repo.

## Audience
Developers and note-takers who already use a notes app and are tired of accounts, sync spinners
and "where does this live?". They know Markdown and ⌘K palettes.

## Where it plays
16:9 1920x1080, 30 fps, 15-20 s (recommended 20). Silent autoplay first: every beat must read
without sound.

## Must include
- Real Tidepool UI (examples/_apps/tidepool), dark and/or light theme, real seed notes only
- Real copy only: "Your notes, kept on your own machine.", "Stays on your device",
  "Sync: local only", "Saved on this device", "Find anything with ⌘K"
- The name lands last; the end card says Tidepool is a fictional app (showtime sample material)

## Must avoid
- Invented numbers, users, quotes, dates or prices; no "coming soon" date (none was given)
- The launch video's opener (01-launch-tidepool uses "No account. No server." cards)
- Generic trailer props (glowing brains, rockets, particle tunnels)

## Concept
C2 "Macro" + C1's sunrise ending (D-002): extreme close-ups of the real app, withheld until a pull-back
on the drop; the app sinks under the logo's wave line, the sun rises, "Tidepool" lands on the final hit.

## Look
Dark app plates (3840x2160), CSS 3D macro camera with shallow focus, teal/amber grade, vignette, grain.
Cards and name in Instrument Serif (C1's type), UI and tagline in Inter (D-006, assumed).

## Sound
cinematic-build, 95 bpm, Dm, 20 s: intro 0, build 7.6, drop 12.6 (riser + impact), outro 15.2, end hit 16.47.
Water drops (hook, tick, name), six soft key clicks, one heavy whoosh on the pull-back, a swell into the name. No VO.

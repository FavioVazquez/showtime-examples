clips/01-one-hell-of-a-job.mp4
"One hell of a job pulling that thing off." Apollo 17 flight director Gene Kranz, who says he was involved in the
beginning of every mission from Mercury to the space station, on following the uncrewed 22-day Artemis I mission.
From NASA's Apollo 17 Legends and Artemis Leaders panel, December 2022.

clips/02-i-just-flew-a-spaceship-around-the-moon.mp4
At seven she saw the flight control room on a tour of Space Center Houston. "I just flew a spaceship around the
moon from that very flight control room." Artemis flight controller Jessi Horelica at NASA's Apollo 17 Legends and
Artemis Leaders panel, December 2022.

clips/03-it-can-be-done-we-did-it-50-years-ago.mp4
What did Apollo do that will help Artemis the most? "The fact that we did it." Apollo 17 lead flight director
Gerry Griffin answers NASA Johnson Space Center Director Vanessa Wyche at the Apollo 17 Legends and Artemis
Leaders panel, December 2022.

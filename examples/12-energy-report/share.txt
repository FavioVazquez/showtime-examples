Where US electricity came from, 1950-2025: coal, gas, wind and solar in EIA's own numbers

YouTube description:
In 2024, US wind and solar farms made more electricity than coal for the first time in EIA's annual record: 672 vs 652 TWh (utility-scale, all sectors). They did it again in 2025 (760 vs 737 TWh), even though coal generation rose 13 % that year.
Coal made about half of US electricity for decades and peaked in 2007 (2,016 TWh, 48.5 %). Natural gas passed coal first, in 2016. In 2025 gas made 40.8 %, nuclear 17.7 %, wind + solar 17.2 %, coal 16.6 %.

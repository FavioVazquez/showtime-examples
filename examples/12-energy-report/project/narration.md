## hook
In twenty twenty-four, America's wind and solar farms made more electricity than coal, for the first time.

## coal-era
For decades, coal made about half of America's power. It peaked in two thousand seven.

## gas
Natural gas took its place first. In twenty sixteen, gas passed coal, and it has stayed ahead.

## newcomers
Wind and solar were under one percent in two thousand seven. Then they climbed past coal in twenty twenty-four, and again in twenty twenty-five.

## race {pause_after=2.2}
Rank six sources year by year: gas takes the top spot, and wind passes hydro.

## mix-2025
In twenty twenty-five, gas made about forty-one percent. Nuclear, wind and solar, and coal each made about a sixth, and coal's output rose thirteen percent.

## source

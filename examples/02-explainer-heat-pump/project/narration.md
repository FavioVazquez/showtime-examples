## hook
It's freezing outside. So how can that air heat your home?

## absolute {pause_after=0.6}
Even freezing air holds heat. Zero Celsius is still far above absolute zero.

## loop {pause_after=0.55}
A heat pump moves that heat indoors, with refrigerant circling a sealed loop.

## evaporator
Outside, refrigerant colder than the air soaks up its heat and boils into a gas.

## compressor
A compressor squeezes the gas, and its temperature shoots up.

## condenser
Indoors, the hot gas releases that heat into your home and condenses to a liquid.

## valve {pause_after=0.6}
An expansion valve drops the pressure, the liquid goes cold, and the loop repeats.

## payoff
Moving heat beats making it. You usually get more heat out than electricity in.

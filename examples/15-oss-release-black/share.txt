Black 26.1.0 (January 18, 2026) introduced the 2026 stable style. Here is what it changes in code you already formatted, shown as real diffs: 26.1.0 run on files that Black 25.12.0 had formatted.

- Redundant parentheses on the left of = are removed (#4865)
- A docstring after a comment is now seen as the module docstring (#4764)
- Type comments become "# type: int" (#4645)
- Also stable: one blank line after imports (#4489), calls on multiline strings stay compact (#1879), generic functions split at the arguments (#4777). 9 features in all; all of it was in --preview during 2025.

pip install -U black (newer 26.x releases keep the 2026 style)

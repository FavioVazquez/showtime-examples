## hello {at=4.3 pause_after=0.6}
This is Tidepool, a notes app that keeps everything on your own device. Let's capture a note from start to finish.

## new {pause_after=0.9}
To start a note, press N. The New note button does the same thing.

## title {pause_after=1.7}
The cursor is already in the title, so just type.

## enter {pause_after=0.5}
Press Enter to jump down to the body.

## body {pause_after=0.9}
The body is Markdown. A dash and two brackets make a checklist item.

## continue {pause_after=2.9}
Press Enter, and the list carries on by itself.

## preview {pause_after=0.5}
In split view, the preview on the right renders it as you type.

## flip {pause_after=1.2}
Command E flips to the full preview. Press it again to keep writing.

## tag {pause_after=1.1}
Tags go under the title. Click Add tag, type planning, and press Enter.

## notebook {pause_after=1.0}
Then choose a notebook. This one belongs in Work.

## saved {pause_after=0.7}
There's no save button. Tidepool saves as you type, and nothing leaves this device.

## sync {pause_after=0.8}
The sidebar says so too: sync is set to local only.

## pin {pause_after=1.6}
Press Escape to leave the editor, then P to pin the note to the top.

## pinned {pause_after=1.2}
Pinned notes stay above everything else, marked with a small pin.

## recap {pause_after=1.2}
That's the whole loop. Press N, type, and Tidepool keeps it.

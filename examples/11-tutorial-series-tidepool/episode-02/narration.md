<!-- Episode 02 · Find anything with search and tags. One line per beat; pause_after leaves room for the action on screen. -->

## hello {at=4.3 pause_after=0.6}
Once you have a few notes, finding the right one should take a second. Here's how.

## open {pause_after=0.8}
Press Command K, or click Search, to open the command palette.

## recent {pause_after=0.5}
Before you type anything, it lists your most recent notes.

## type {pause_after=0.9}
Type any word. Tidepool searches titles, text and tags at once.

## match {pause_after=0.4}
Matches are highlighted, and the best one comes first.

## pick {pause_after=1.8}
Use the arrow keys to choose a result, then press Enter to open it.

## tags {pause_after=0.9}
Start with a hash sign, and the palette lists your tags instead.

## filter {pause_after=0.8}
Press Enter, and the list shows only the notes with that tag.

## jk {pause_after=1.4}
With the list in focus, J and K step through the notes. No mouse needed.

## narrow {pause_after=1.1}
To narrow it further, type in Filter this list. Escape clears it again.

## sidebar {pause_after=1.4}
The sidebar works too. Click any notebook or tag to filter with one click.

## commands {pause_after=2.0}
Finally, a greater-than sign turns search into commands. Let's try dark mode.

## dark {pause_after=0.6}
Every note, tag and command is one shortcut away.

## recap {pause_after=1.2}
So: Command K searches everything, a hash jumps to tags, and greater-than runs commands.

## outro
Your notes stay on your device, and now they're easy to find.

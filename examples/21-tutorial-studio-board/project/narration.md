## preview {pause_after=0.65}
In one minute, you'll have picked a direction, and left a note.

## open-cmd
Step one: open the board. Claude runs showtime studio open, and gives you a private, local link.

## open-board {pause_after=0.65}
The link opens the board in your browser. Each concept is a card, with style frames, a hook, a palette, and a music sketch.

## compare-frames
Step two: compare. Click the thumbnails to step through a concept's style frames.

## compare-bed
Press play to hear its music sketch: ten seconds of the bed it would use.

## compare-wipe {pause_after=0.65}
Further down, Compare puts two concepts side by side. Drag across the frame to wipe from one to the other.

## react-like
Step three: react. Press L to like the concept in focus, or click the heart.

## react-note
Press C to leave a note, in your own words. Rough is fine.

## react-send {pause_after=0.65}
Control Enter sends it. Every reaction is saved to the job as you go.

## pick-click
Step four: pick. Click Pick on the direction you want.

## pick-review
Press the full stop key to review everything you've sent.

## pick-director {pause_after=0.65}
Then tell Claude you're done. It runs studio feedback, reads your pick and your note, and builds from your choice.

## recap
Open, compare, react, pick. The full guide is the studio reference, in the showtime skill.

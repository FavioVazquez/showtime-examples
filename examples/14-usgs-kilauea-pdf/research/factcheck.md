# Fact check and license audit: Kīlauea 2018 (EN + ES)

Done inline by the director following references/crew/researcher.md (no sub-agent tool in this session).

## Blockers
None. The contract's early wording was corrected before the script: "In 2018" (not "summer"), no "three months",
716 attributed to Hawaiʻi County, "May 3: fissure eruptions begin", collapses as M5 *energy*.

## Verified (18 claims, all scenes)
See claims.verified.json. Every on-screen figure matches the PDF text exactly (13.7, 716, 875, ~60,000, 500+ m,
M6.9, ≈ M5, dates). Computed conversions: 354 ha (875 acres), "about 60 m" (200 ft, ES card only).

## Not claimed (checked)
No deaths, evacuations, dollar damage, SO2, "largest ever"; no USGS endorsement; no USGS identifier on screen
(PDF page 2 used, page 1 with the logo not shown; the clips' corner "USGS" bug cropped by the page's 1.12-1.14x
framing, verified on full-size frames at 0, 3.4, 6.5, 42.5, 44.5, 46 and 49.5 s).

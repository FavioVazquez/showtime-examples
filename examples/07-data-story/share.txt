GitHub README caption: A 30-second data story made with showtime from NASA's GISTEMP v4 annual global temperature anomalies.
Alt text: Warming stripes for 1880 to 2025, blue in early years turning deep red recently, fold into a bar chart of yearly temperature differences from the 1951–1980 average; the last 11 bars are the tallest, and 2024 is highlighted at +1.29 °C.

Notes for posting:
- Values are NASA's as downloaded on 2026-09-26; GISTEMP is revised as new station data arrives, so recent values can shift by a few hundredths.
- 2026 is left out because its annual mean is not complete.
- The stripes are drawn from the data by the video itself (after Ed Hawkins' "warming stripes" idea); no third-party graphic is used.
- Frame 0 is already the hook (headline over the stripes), so chat apps and autoplay show it. Where the platform takes a cover image (YouTube, X, LinkedIn), upload poster.jpg (the 3.0 s frame).

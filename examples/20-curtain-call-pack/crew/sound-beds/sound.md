# Sound board (sound designer, done inline by the director: no sub-agent tool in this session)

Four 10 s beds, one per concept, all composed locally with `showtime audio compose` (no library items, nothing to
credit), in D to match the brand sound logo's key, mastered to -14 LUFS / -1 dBTP.

| id | style | key | pairs with | extra |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bed-c1 | piano-emotional | D | C1 Velvet curtain | `logo-sting` motif (D) with its tonic at 6.0 s (the landing moment) |
| bed-c2 | synthwave | D minor | C2 Marquee | drop at 5.0 s |
| bed-c3 | ambient-pad | D | C3 Spotlight | `swell` (D) peaking into a `thock` at 6.0 s |
| bed-w1 | retro-8bit | D | W1 Arcade | - |

Recommended: bed-c1 (the landing is the brand moment; the logo motif sits on it).

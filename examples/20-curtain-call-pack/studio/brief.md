# Curtain Call motion pack: studio brief

Current truth for this job. The history lives in decisions.md. The build reads this file.

## Contract
A 5 s Curtain Call logo sting (16:9, 1:1, 9:16, native layouts) with a sound logo, plus four lower thirds and
two transition stingers with alpha that an editor can drop into Premiere, all in the showtime brand.

## Audience
Editors and makers who put showtime's brand at the start or end of a video; viewers in feeds (the sting's
frame 0 must read as showtime).

## Where it plays
Sting: 16:9 1920x1080, 1:1 1080x1080, 9:16 1080x1920, 5.0 s, -14 LUFS / -1 dBTP. Lower thirds: 1920x1080,
6 s, ProRes 4444 alpha .mov + VP9 alpha .webm. Stingers: 1920x1080, 1 s, alpha. Web: embeddable HTML sting,
hero loop (webp + gif), web MP4.

## Must include
- The official mark (hero SVG as the face of an extruded tile) and the outlined Fraunces 900 wordmark.
- Sound logo in D on the landing.

## Must avoid
- Velvet as text on dark (2.7:1), gold on cream, a rotated/stretched mark at rest, effects on the mark or wordmark.
- Replacing assets/brand/motion/sting.mp4 (v1 stays; adopting v2 is a later decision).

## Concept
C1 "Velvet curtain" (D-002): three.js stage; curtains part; the mark is flown in and lands in the pool.

## Look
The C1 frames: Stage ground, velvet curtains with sheen and a gold hem, pleated valance, gold beam with dust,
cream wordmark on the dark apron (D-007).

## Sound
Piano warmth (bed-c1) under a new logo-sting motif in D, tonic on the landing (D-003); whoosh for the curtain
travel, thock on the landing, shimmer on the wordmark.

## Storyboard
0-0.3 slit of light · 0.3-2.3 curtains part, dolly in 7 % · 2.05-2.8 mark flown in, lands at 2.8 on the tonic ·
3.05 wordmark wipes up, readable from 3.85, holds to 5.0.

## Preferences

- Questions: at most 5 per round.

Small on purpose.

Three things, done well.

Stays on your device

Notes are saved in this browser’s local storage. There is no sign-up, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Find anything with ⌘K

One palette searches titles, text and #tags as you type, and runs commands too. Your hands stay on the keyboard.

Markdown, previewed live

Write plain text. Headings, task lists, tables and code render side by side while you type.
